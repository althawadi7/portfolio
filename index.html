ABOUT

Two crafts, one instinct for detail

I spent the first part of my career behind a camera and inside an edit bay — cinematography, VFX, and 3D animation for production houses and broadcast TV. It taught me to think in systems: every frame, layer, and shot has to hold together under a deadline.

In 2023 I retrained through a full-time Software Engineering Immersive and moved into full-stack development. Since then I've built backend systems, SaaS platforms, and mobile apps professionally — carrying that same eye for craft into Python, PHP, Node.js, Go, and Flutter.

Today I design REST APIs, real-time systems, and payment integrations for products that real people use — across 20+ delivered projects in blockchain, ecommerce, government, and fintech.
